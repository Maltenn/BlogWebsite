The only people of color in his life worked for him and had no choice but to muster words of admiration. History was repeating itself in front of me, proof of how easily inequality goes unnoticed without the historical context to see it. As my resentment of the evangelical Republican area I grew up in deepened, so did my disdain for the system and for the people who supported it. I would speak up on occasion with my family and explain why I thought the narratives they promoted were harmful and hateful, but all it did was distance me from them. This is where my logic and my emotions stop aligning the way they usually do. Morally, this group of people holds beliefs I would reject from anyone else in my life. Why should blood make them any different? You might think, from the way I’ve framed this piece, that I don’t love my family.
