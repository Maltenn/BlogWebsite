I had been handed a direct line to my emotions, but that still didn't answer what began this multi-year spiral. What's the point? My purpose is clear to me now: to create, and to hand others even a portion of the help I so desperately needed. I want to learn as many art forms as I can and use them to connect someone to themselves, or to one another. The more I create, the more reasons I find to believe it is the most valuable thing I can do with my life. Art can turn the world's eyes toward the inequalities and atrocities that plague it. It can carry stories, feelings, thoughts, and awareness of heavy subjects to the people willing to let art into their souls. I could spend pages listing the reasons I have learned, through the process, that creating matters. None of them answers the question. Why do I create?
