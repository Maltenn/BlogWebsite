My brain filled with thoughts faster than I could process them. Thoughts of how few visits I have left with my grandparents, only to be replaced by thoughts of who I am. Maybe they didn’t have a complete understanding of me, as it had seemed, but at least they had a cohesive theory. I was nowhere near that. I found myself guilty again. The physical distance had made it easy not to be intentional about my relationship with them, and let me be lazily, emotionally distant. Meanwhile they were still living by the principles they had taught me, showing me nothing but kindness and generosity. How could I be so selfish as to harbor even an ounce of resentment for people who had shown me this much love at every stage of my life? All these questions slammed against the walls of my skull. Overwhelmed with guilt, with sorrow, and, for the first time in a long time, with the feeling of being seen, I cried.
