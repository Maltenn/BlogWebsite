I was embraced in a way that reminded me of something I had long forgotten. I was loved. In the moments before, I had been anxious that they would make fun of the septum piercing I had gotten since they last saw me, and those thoughts melted away, though not for good. Throughout the night I would catch them making eye contact with it instead of me. But I stopped caring, because as the night went on, there wasn’t a single lull in the conversation. We picked up as if I had seen them last week. We talked about all the same things we used to when I saw them most weeks: tennis, food we had made recently, funny stories about our friends, and everything else that came to mind. What I didn’t expect was that, over the course of the evening, they knew something I no longer did. They knew who I used to be.
