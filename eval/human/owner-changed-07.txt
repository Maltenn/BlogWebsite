You make them laugh. You work hard. I still carry those into every interaction I have, at least the ones outside of games where my bot lane is 1/9. Growing up, I wanted to be like my grandfather. Everyone liked and admired him, or so it seemed through the children’s telescope I observed him with, a lens too young to understand what it was seeing. As I grew older, my understanding of politics, of the world around me, and of a history that has been unkind to most people grew with me. Perspectives settled into place. Privileges that had always been with me, invisible for their constancy, came into focus once I went looking for answers to the questions I had. “40 acres and a mule” was never just a saying. It was another wound, still left unhealed. Now I realize that not everyone liked or admired my grandfather. His fellow rich white peers did.
