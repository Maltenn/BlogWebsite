It is unfortunately much more complicated. I am overwhelmingly guilty for loving them. I resent them, and I so desperately want their approval. I go looking for justifications, and I find statistics that quiet my conscience. The one I default to is that the single best predictor of someone’s political alignment is not their race, their gender, or their financial status. It is the political alignment of the people who raised them. I remind myself that my grandparents are far less racist than the people who raised them, and I use that to justify an evening spent with them. An evening with them meant Family Feud on the TV, where we made fun of the contestants floundering through pitiful answers and seemingly lost common sense. But even with my attempts at justification, I began to resent nights like these as I started to hear the undertones I had once been too naïve to understand.
