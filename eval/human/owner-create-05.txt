I struggle with this to this day. I don't feel big emotions anymore. Good, bad, infuriating, delightful, and everything in between, all of it lands with the same faint, polite flicker you would feel at a B+ on a test you expected a B- on, or the reverse. Searching for any way to feel again, I did what I always seem to do and lucked into a woman smarter than me, who guided me along. Lucy introduced me to poetry. I liked her voice, and I liked it even more when she read poetry aloud. Some poems, not all, were followed by small discussions about what we loved and what we didn't. Finally, a spark had been lit again. The coals of my soul, ones I thought had gone cold, were met with the oxygen of Lucy's breath, and caught fire on the pages we read, the first flame I had felt in as long as I could remember.
