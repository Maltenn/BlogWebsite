What if I lay on my deathbed one day, turned away from heaven over a sentence I said at twelve? That fear didn't last. I have always loved to talk. Losing my faith left a hole, and into it fell a question I had never been confronted with: well then, what's the fucking point? Why should I care about anything? I would die one day, and shortly after, I would be forgotten. That didn't frighten me. It left me apathetic toward the world around me. Everything drained to grey, and the only color that survived the draining was a worrying one: red. These weren't the rose-tinted glasses the movies talk about. What little feeling I could still muster came out as anger. I was already self-conscious, swimming in hormones I didn't understand, and now I had no purpose to anchor any of it. I sank. Smiles and laughs grew fewer and farther between.
