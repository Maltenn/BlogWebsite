I devoured it. I never went looking for facts. Surely nothing on the internet could settle, once and for all, whether Christianity was real or false. What I could do was listen to people who had wrestled with it for far longer than I had, people far wiser than me, and hear what they had made of the struggle. A month or so passed, and I realized I already knew what I believed. I was just too scared to admit it. Still afraid of punishment, I pulled away by degrees, the way you back out of a room without turning your back on whatever is inside it. First, I stopped reading scripture. Then I prayed once a week instead of every night. Then, eventually, not at all. Even after I had given up every habit and practice, I couldn't say it out loud to anyone. What if saying it aloud meant I could never take it back?
