It is easier to come to terms with anything when you have a scapegoat. That was the easy way out. I sort my emotions into boxes, and it was only a short step to sorting people into them. It is incredibly selfish to take people who have loved me despite every flaw I have, lump them into a box of the flawed, and leave it in the attic to collect dust. It is my responsibility to treat everyone with love and kindness. It is my responsibility to understand that it is not okay that my grandfather made a game of trying to hit the Black kids who reset the pins at the bowling alley, instead of aiming for strikes. But I also have to understand that had the environment he grew up in been the one I grew up in, I would bet he would be much different. It is hard to find the line between understanding why my family is the way they are and mistaking that understanding for a valid excuse for hateful behavior.
