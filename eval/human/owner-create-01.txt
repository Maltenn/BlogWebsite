Why do I create? Why would I spend two years of my life refining a website that no one asked for, that will only cost me money to run, and that time will one day swallow without a sound? Like every answer I give, it’s long winded, and I’ll find a way to tie it back to my childhood. When I was in middle school, the first girl I ever loved began to open my eyes to everything I had never questioned about myself and the way I was raised. Her name was Lucy. Christianity made no logical sense, and I had never once examined it. I was taught that questioning religion was the devil's trick, that if I fell for it, I would burn in hell. I was always curious, but more than curious, I was afraid. Understanding seemed a poor trade for eternal damnation, and I was not willing to gamble.
