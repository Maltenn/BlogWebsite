It was the chance to start over, to clear my browser history of self. No one here had known me since childhood. I could become a better, newer version of myself and finally be free of the person I so desperately tried to leave behind. Over a year later, still excruciatingly far from my unachievable goals, I looked back on the year before and admitted that another year of changing who I was had passed. But this year felt different. In years prior, when I turned retrospective, what had changed were my thoughts, hobbies, interests, and understanding of concepts. This year, more had changed than what lived inside my own head. I was no longer in the same small town. My friends were an entirely different set of people than they had been for all the years before. I struggle to remember what the roads look like back in Georgia, and the days between memories grow longer and longer.
