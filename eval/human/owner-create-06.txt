One book led to two, then to ten. Before I knew it, my social media was nothing but poetry. These authors wielded words in ways I would never have thought of until my eyes were opened. Their metaphors joined ideas and feelings that, before poetry, had lived in separate rooms of my mind, and the walls came down. A phrase I heard a few years back, and I'm embarrassed to say where, is how I explain my fascination: "Poetry is the most economical form of language." It is why poetry called to me even at that age. The fewest possible words, the biggest possible emotion. For a boy bankrupt of feeling, a language that spent so little and bought so much was irresistible. I remain envious of those capable of portraying their perspectives laid bare so elegantly. Capable of securing returns that the elite would commit more heinous crimes than they already do to achieve.
