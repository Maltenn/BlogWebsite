I hated who I was back there, and I was finally free. I don’t get homesick, because I was sick of home. The water back there was poisoned with the same chemicals that had been wearing me down all my life, and when I returned for a brief visit, it took only a few sips to remind me why I left. I missed my family, but I didn’t miss living with them. It was a sacrifice I had been willing to make, a trade finally on my terms, and how naïve I was to think I had gotten it right on the first try. I have a love for logic and understanding. My emotions follow logic, which, unfortunately for some, leaves me not too dissimilar to a robot. The one place I have never managed to align the two is my grandparents. They raised me with a few non-negotiables. You show people kindness and generosity.
