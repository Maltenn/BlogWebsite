I finally had a purpose again. I had resigned myself to the loss of my ability to care about anything, and this slapped me awake. I struggle to feel my emotions every single week of my life, but I know that art will always be the road back to my soul. My emotions might be absent most days, but this I now know, they can always drop by for a visit, like an old friend you missed, but didn’t realize how much until they stood in front of you. It was the beginning of the rest of my life. Consuming art and learning the emotions that powered it became a drug, and I needed it the way you need anything that gives you access to a part of yourself you long believed was dead. The love didn't stop at poetry. It spread, hungrily, to makeup, fashion, animation, music, painting, drawing, photography, videography, modern art, experimental art, and a much longer list that I hope never stops growing.
