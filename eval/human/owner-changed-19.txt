It is my job to love them despite their flaws: not to make excuses for those flaws, and not to resent them for those flaws either. The second lesson was that you cannot find yourself if you never stay the same. It doesn’t matter which wooden plank, once replaced, made it no longer Theseus’s ship if the boat never set sail. What use is a boat if all you ever do is repair it and never take it out to enjoy what you’ve built? Who cares if you are changing board after board with no end in sight? It is an ouroboros of self-improvement, pointless if you never get to live a life in the improved state.
