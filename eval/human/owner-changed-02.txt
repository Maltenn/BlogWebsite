In its original formulation, the paradox asks whether a ship that has had every component replaced, one by one, remains the same ship. When it was first posed to me, I hadn’t dipped a toe into philosophy, so for years I understood it through my hobby of building computers. I got my first prebuilt, a $400 machine, back in 2014, and I have been obsessed with modifying it ever since. I have replaced each part, slowly, one by one, over years and more than once. Five different graphics cards, three motherboards, three cases, and the list goes on. I never changed it all at once. Each swap came when a bottleneck revealed itself and I had a little extra cash from making sandwiches at my first job. My roughly $3,000 computer is clearly not the one I started with, but when did it stop being that one? After the very last original part was pulled?
