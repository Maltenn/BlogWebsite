went to the farmers market this morning and ended up talking to the honey guy for like twenty minutes. he has bees on his roof in the middle of the city which I did not know was a thing you could just do. apparently they fly up to three miles looking for flowers so his honey tastes different every season depending on what's blooming. the spring batch is light and kind of floral and the late summer one is darker, almost like molasses. I bought both obviously. he also said the biggest problem isn't the bees, it's the neighbors who panic every time one lands on their balcony. made me think about how little I know about where any of my food comes from. anyway I'm going back next week to ask him about the wax candles he sells because they smelled amazing and I have zero self control at markets.
