Great question! Starting a sourdough journey can feel overwhelming at first, but with a little patience, you'll be baking delicious loaves in no time. Here are a few tips to help you get started.

First, focus on your starter. A healthy, active starter is the foundation of any great loaf. Feed it consistently at the same time each day, and keep it somewhere warm, ideally around 75 to 78 degrees. You'll know it's ready when it doubles in size within four to six hours of feeding.

Second, don't be afraid of a wet dough. Higher hydration doughs can be tricky to handle, but they produce that beautiful open crumb everyone loves. Using wet hands and a bench scraper can make shaping much easier.

Additionally, it's crucial to give your dough enough time to proof. Under-proofed bread can be dense and gummy, while over-proofed dough may collapse in the oven. The poke test is a reliable way to check.

Remember, every loaf is a learning experience. Embrace the process, take notes, and most importantly, enjoy the journey. Happy baking!
