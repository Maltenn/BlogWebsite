I totally get where you're coming from, and honestly I went through something really similar last year. When you first start learning guitar, it feels like your fingers will never cooperate and every chord change takes forever. The key thing that helped me was practicing just ten or fifteen minutes every single day instead of trying to cram in a two hour session on the weekend. Consistency really does matter more than intensity, especially in the beginning when you're building calluses and muscle memory. Another tip is to pick songs you actually love, even if they're a little above your level, because staying motivated is half the battle. I also found it super helpful to slow everything down with a metronome and only speed up once I could play cleanly. And don't compare yourself to people on the internet who have been playing for twenty years! Everyone starts somewhere. You've got this, just keep at it and in a few months you'll be surprised how far you've come.
