📝 Why do we create?
post #001 — the first one ever. historic.

so i made a website. and somewhere between centering a div and downloading my fourth animated gif of a construction worker, i started asking myself: why do we create anything at all?

like, nobody asked for this site. the internet was fine without it. there are a billion websites and most of them load faster than mine and have fewer patricks on them. and yet here i am at 3am, hand-placing sparkles, feeling something dangerously close to joy.

i think that's the answer honestly. you don't create because the world needs it. you create because you need it. because having a little corner of the internet that is exactly, aggressively, unapologetically YOURS is one of the last pure things left. no algorithm. no feed. just vibes and a hit counter.

also i needed somewhere to post my top 10 lists. that was like 70% of it.
