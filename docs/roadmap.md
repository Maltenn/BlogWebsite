# Not done yet (on purpose, or next)

Each item says where its code will go. Anything that needs storage also adds a numbered file in `migrations/`, and anything that switches on with secrets follows the rules in [CONVENTIONS.md](../CONVENTIONS.md).

- **Spotify for more than 5 people.** This needs Spotify's extended quota (see [deploy.md](deploy.md)), which is out of reach for a small site. Alternatives would be letting people paste a song or playlist link (no sign-in needed), or Last.fm (or Apple Music). They go in `src/music/`, next to `spotify.js`: `links.js` for pasted links, `lastfm.js` for Last.fm.
- **Changing screen names.** `src/accounts/names.js`.
- **Video: dropped.** Photos are built (phase 4: statuses, replies, quotes, threads, comments, blog covers and uploaded profile pictures; see [architecture.md](architecture.md#photos)). The owner dropped video clips, so [decisions/005](decisions/005-media-formats.md)'s video pipeline won't be built.
- **Automatic CSAM scanning, when the site grows.** Launch is in-house review (the Photos queue, `csam` reports that hold photos, the 48-hour clocks). Upgrade by applying for Microsoft PhotoDNA Cloud or the Google Content Safety API (free to approved organisations; covers every photo, private ones too; needs code in `src/media/` on upload), or by serving public photos through a cached R2 custom domain so Cloudflare's free CSAM Scanning Tool sees them (no approval; public photos only; needs code, `img-src` and cache purges). Details in [deploy.md](deploy.md#photos-csam-detection-launch-in-house-review).
- **Pictures inside blog posts.** A blog post has a cover photo only. Inline images would go in the blog body's marks (`public/js/widgets/blogtext.js`), with the uploads attached as `media` like a status's, in `src/social/social.js`.
- **Serving photos from their own domain.** Photos come from the site's own origin, which the strict headers on `/media/` make safe; [decisions/005](decisions/005-media-formats.md) prefers a separate domain. Moving them means a second custom domain on the Worker, `img-src` in `public/_headers`, and the URLs in `src/shared/media.js`.
- **Unread threads on the boards.** Boards (phase 3) are built without read state. Bold unread threads that open at the first unread comment need a per-thread marker, `thread_reads(user_id, thread_id, last_post_id)`, moved forward only like the feed's (see [decisions/006](decisions/006-twitter-shape-ux.md), "Later phases at a glance"). It goes in `src/boards/`, as `reads.js`.
- **Notification housekeeping.** Notifications stay until what they're about is deleted (or the account goes). If the table grows large, a line in the cron in `src/index.js` can drop read ones older than a few months.
- **Real-time chat and presence.** Everything polls now (the inbox every few seconds, badges every 30s). The replacement is `src/live/`, a Durable Object that carries events and owns no data, plus `public/js/core/live.js` on the page, replacing the polling.
- **Email verification at sign-up.** Addresses are only used for password resets for now. Sending a confirmation link first would stop people signing up with someone else's address. `src/accounts/verify.js`.
- **Terms of service.** `/rules` has plain-language rules and a privacy summary. Have them read by someone qualified before opening sign-ups widely, especially since the site stores birthdays and allows 13-year-olds. The terms are static pages in `public/legal/` (add `/legal/` to `STATIC_PREFIXES` in the router), and the server's part goes in `src/accounts/terms.js`.
- **Ban evasion.** A banned person can't log in, but can make a new account. The per-IP sign-up limit slows that down, and invite codes would stop it. Invite codes go in `src/accounts/invites.js`; other signals for spotting a returning banned person go in `src/safety/`.
